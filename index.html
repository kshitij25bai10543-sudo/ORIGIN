THE PROBLEM

India's forests are changing. The data is fragmented.

FRA implementation generates information across multiple departments and systems. When these datasets remain disconnected, delays and inconsistencies become difficult to identify.

01 FRA Claims Applications, approvals, rejections and pending claims.
02 Land Records Claimed area compared with recorded land information.
03 District Data Geographic and administrative information.
04 State Reports Aggregated progress and implementation indicators.
